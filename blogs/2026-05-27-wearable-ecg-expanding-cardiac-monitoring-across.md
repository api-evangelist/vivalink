Wearable ECG Expanding Cardiac Monitoring Across Therapeutic Applications Cardiac monitoring plays a key role in safeguarding heart health, especially in cardiology research and pharmaceutical trials. Now, new research is demonstrating how cardiac monitoring is instrumental to research well beyond its original contexts, transforming how sponsors use this tool in trials . A prime example is a recent study conducted by Ochsner Health, which directly illustrated the potential of using cardiac monitoring to examine the relationship between seizures and cardiac function in epilepsy patients.
