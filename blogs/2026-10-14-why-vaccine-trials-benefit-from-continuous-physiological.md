Why Vaccine Clinical Trials Benefit from Continuous Physiological Monitoring One of the most persistent challenges in vaccine clinical trials is generating precise, timely data across a large and often widely dispersed participant population. Reactogenicity events such as fever, injection site reactions, and fatigue typically emerge and resolve within 24 to 72 hours of dosing, a timeline that often outpaces paper diaries and scheduled follow-up calls.
