The Advantages of Cardiac Monitoring in Supporting Chemotherapy The most effective chemotherapy drugs pose significant cardiac risks to cancer patients, however traditional monitoring methods are limited to periodic clinic visits. These visits cannot account for the day to day activities of cancer patient’s lives, leaving gaps in the data clinicians need. Wearable ECG technology offers a way to keep cancer patients safer without disrupting their daily lives, while simultaneously giving clinicians that critical data.
